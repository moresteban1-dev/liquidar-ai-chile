# Growth & Viral Marketing Mastery

This skill focuses on the bridge between technical excellence and user acquisition. It combines high-conversion page design with creative marketing strategies.

## 1. Landing Page Precision

Every element exists to drive a single action.

* **5-Second Rule**: Answer "What/Why/How" above the fold.
* **Visual Hierarchy**: Use Z-pattern layouts for minimal landing pages.
* **Trust Triangle**: Combine Personal Social Proof, Authority Logos, and Risk Reversal (Guarantees).

### Conversion Frameworks

- **PAS**: Problem-Agitate-Solve.
* **BAB**: Before-After-Bridge.
* **AIDA**: Attention-Interest-Desire-Action.

## 2. Viral Marketing & Acquisition

Designing experiments that spread naturally.

* **Viral Loop Design**: Build features that incentivize sharing as part of the core experience.
* **FOMO & Urgency**: Use scarcity and time-limited offers ethically to drive action.
* **Micro-Scripts**: Develop small, shareable tools (name generators, calculators) that act as "lead magnets".

## 3. Optimization Checklist

| Section | Goal |
|---------|------|
| **Hero** | Capture attention with an emotional hook. |
| **Social Proof** | Verify with testimonials and logos. |
| **Solution** | Present product as the ultimate relief. |
| **Final CTA** | One clear button with zero friction. |

## 4. Anti-Patterns

- ❌ **Multiple Competing CTAs**: Never confuse the user with too many choices.
* ❌ **Vague Copy**: Avoid "Welcome to our site" - focus on "Get [Benefit] Now".
* ❌ **Missing Proof**: Never assume the user trusts you; show them evidence.
